01 / WHY IT IS PAID

The application is free. Hosting has continuing costs.

Local Flight itself remains free and open source. Bring Your Own Keys and VATSIM remain available without Relay Access.

Service Provider-authorized aviation data

Hosted real-flight data must be licensed and operated independently from the open-source app.

Operation Servers, delivery, and protection

Payments, license delivery, abuse protection, infrastructure, and maintenance continue after a download.

Model Paid directly, without tracking

Charging for hosting helps avoid advertising, behavioral tracking, selling usage data, or requiring an account for the desktop software.

02 / SERVICE BOUNDARIES

Only the feature you choose uses this path.

Local screens stay local.

Desktop windows, LAN browsers, LED matrices, and Companion phones connect to your own Local Flight computer. Beacon Relay is not in that screen connection.

/health · JSON →

Hosted data is optional.

Beacon Relay receives an allowed Local Flight request, applies update limits and privacy protections, then returns shared airport data. Hosted data needs Relay Access, sold separately.

Remote messages stay unreadable.

The encryption key stays on the paired phone and computer. Beacon Relay passes along the encrypted message, so Beacon Tools cannot read the request or response.

Notice / informational only

For display and hobby use only. Local Flight and relay data may be delayed, incomplete, cached, wrong, or unavailable. Do not use them for navigation, dispatch, operational control, flight planning, professional aviation work, or safety decisions.
